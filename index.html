📹 Cloud Video Surveillance & Technology Policing

Taiwan has built one of the densest municipal closed-circuit television (CCTV) and automated license plate recognition (ALPR) networks in the world. Street robbers and motorcycle bag-snatchers face virtually instant detection and tracking across intersections, elevating the clearance rate to 98%–99.5% and deterring opportunists.

💳 The Cashless Society Shift

Between 2015 and 2025, mobile payment penetration (Line Pay, JKO Pay, Apple Pay, Taiwan Pay) and contactless transit cards drastically reduced physical currency handled by citizens and small retail shops. With fewer cash targets on the street, physical robbery yielded diminishing returns for offenders.

🔄 Understanding 2024–2025 Dynamics

2024 recorded an all-time historical low of 151 robbery cases. The slight uptick to 248 cases in 2025 (+97 cases) reflects post-pandemic mobility normalization and enhanced statistical reporting precision, but remains 66.5% lower than the 740 cases recorded in 2015.

💻 The Pivot to Fraud & Cybercrime

While physical street crime dropped by over 50%, organized criminal elements shifted into telecommunications, crypto, and investment scams (詐欺), which grew +127%. In response, Taiwan enacted the 2024 Anti-Fraud Special Act (打詐專法) to pivot police resources toward financial intelligence and cyber enforcement.
